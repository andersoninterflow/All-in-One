## 2024-09-07 - Contextual ARIA labels in lists
**Learning:** In lists of items with repeated action buttons (like 'Abrir suporte', 'Avaliar'), screen reader users need context. Appending the item's title/identifier via a template literal provides unique, descriptive context.
**Action:** Use template literals for ARIA labels in repeated action buttons to ensure they uniquely identify the subject of the action.
